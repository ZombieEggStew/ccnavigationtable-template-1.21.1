## Changes

- Uses the new [Servo Bearing](../actuators/servo-bearing.md), integrating the ailerons and elevator — simplifying the aero and drive layout
- Auto-roll gained an integral term, eliminating the steady-state error

- Uses the new [Engine](../engine/overview.md) — no extra mods needed
- The control program manages engine temperature and mixture automatically, greatly reducing fuel consumption

## Parameters

- Mass: 22 kg
- Max cruise altitude: 254 blocks
- Max cruise speed: 42 blocks/s
- Fuel consumption: ~100 mb of chocolate per 10,000 blocks

## Flight Operations

1. Turn the **Tgt_alt** knob to the target altitude
2. Flip the **auto_th** switch on
3. Take off
